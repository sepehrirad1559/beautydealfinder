What we do

Beauty Deal Finder is an independent price-comparison site for beauty products. We pull prices from multiple retailers — including Amazon, Sephora, Ulta Beauty, Beautylish, Glossier, and Cult Beauty — and show them side by side, so you can find the best price before you buy. We don't sell products, hold inventory, or process payments ourselves; when you're ready to buy, we send you directly to the retailer to complete your purchase.

How pricing works

Prices are pulled directly from each retailer's official product data — either through their licensed advertising API or an approved affiliate network feed. We don't scrape retailer websites. Prices can change on the retailer's side faster than we can refresh, so we always recommend confirming the final price on the retailer's own page before checking out.

How we make money

Some links on this Site are affiliate links. If you click through and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission from the retailer or their affiliate network, at no extra cost to you. This never affects which retailer we display as the best price — see our Terms of Service for details.
