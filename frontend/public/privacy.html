BeautyDealFinder.org ("we," "us," "Beauty Deal Finder") operates beautydealfinder.com (the "Site"), a price-comparison service for beauty products. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we never collect or process payment information — all purchases happen on the retailer's own site after you click through.

When you click a "Buy at" link, we log: the product and retailer clicked, an anonymous session identifier, your browser's user agent string, the referring page, and a salted one-way hash of your IP address (not the IP address itself). This is used to measure which products and retailers perform well and to reconcile affiliate commissions with each retailer's network — it is not used to identify you personally, and we cannot reverse the hash back to your IP.

We may use standard web server logs and basic analytics (e.g. page views) to understand site usage and fix problems.

Cookies and similar technology

See our Cookie Notice for details on what we use and how to control it.

Third-party retailer links

Clicking a product sends you to the retailer's own website (e.g. Amazon) to complete your purchase. Once you leave our Site, that retailer's own privacy policy governs — we don't control and aren't responsible for their data practices. Some of these links are affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no extra cost to you.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve the Site (understanding what's searched for, what's clicked)
  • To attribute affiliate commissions correctly to BeautyDealFinder.org
  • To detect and prevent abuse (e.g. automated click fraud)

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share the data described above with third parties except: the affiliate networks/retailers needed to attribute a click to a commission, and service providers who host or operate the Site on our behalf (e.g. our hosting provider), under confidentiality obligations.

Data retention

Click records are retained for 24 months for analytics and commission-reconciliation purposes, then deleted or fully anonymized.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

Since we don't collect information that identifies you personally, there's generally nothing to access, correct, or delete on an individual basis. If you have questions about what's collected, contact us at sepehrirad15@gmail.com.
